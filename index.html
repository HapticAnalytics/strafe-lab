Strafe/Lab

Tracking sensitivity finder for Apex Legends · MnK

A blind sensitivity test built around Apex fights. It sweeps you from fast wrist-aim sens to slow arm-aim sens across eight aim skills, records every mouse event, zooms in on your best zone, and reports a recommended range with a 95% interval. It says so when the data can't pick a winner.

    Calibration

    Each test captures raw mouse counts and timestamps the same way the drills do. Repeat each test 3 times; the spread tells you how much is your hand and how much is the input.

    1 · Ruler DPI test

    Put a ruler beside your mousepad. Line the mouse edge up with 0, start, slide it straight to the distance mark, then press Space or click.

    2 · Physical 360° test

    In the Apex Firing Range, mark two points on your pad that give exactly one 360° turn at your current sens. Here, start, move from mark to mark, then press Space or click. Target: within 1%.

    3 · Input timing test

    Start, then move the mouse in steady circles for 5 seconds. Measures polling rate, whether events are frame-aligned, and integrity counters.

    4 · ADS per optic

    Apex's per-optic zoom scaling isn't published, so measure it. In the Firing Range, ADS with one optic and mark a pad distance that turns exactly 360°. Here, pick that optic, start, make the same swipe, and press Space or click.

      Baseline & noise floor

      Your noise floor is how much each number changes from one day to the next when nothing has changed. A later improvement only counts if it is bigger than the smallest detectable change (SDC95 = 1.96 × √2 × day-to-day SD).

      Firing Range protocol

      1. Same legend and the same weapon every time: R-99 with no attachments (or write your exact kit in Notes).
      2. Use the Firing Range dummy settings to make the dummies strafe, and use the same settings every time. Write the exact settings in Notes the first time.
      3. Stand at one fixed, marked spot and distance. Note it.
      4. Record the attempt with your usual capture tool. For 60 seconds, hipfire only, deal as much damage as you can to the strafing dummies.
      5. Count total damage from the recording and enter it below as "R-99 60 s strafe damage" in dmg. Keep the video; note its file name.

      R5 Reloaded (optional) and aim trainer

      Use the same mode, map, settings and scenario every time and note them. For an aim trainer, enter the scenario name as the measure and its score.
