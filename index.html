Strafe/Lab

Tracking sensitivity finder for Apex Legends · MnK

A blind sensitivity test built around Apex fights. It sweeps you from fast wrist-aim sens to slow arm-aim sens across eight aim skills, records every mouse event, zooms in on your best zone, and reports a recommended range with a 95% interval. It says so when the data can't pick a winner.

Your setup

Current: — cm/360

  • Use your real mouse, mousepad and desk setup. Slow sens blocks need room to move your arm.
  • Turn off Windows "Enhance pointer precision". The test asks the browser for raw input and tells you if it got it.
  • Pick Quick, Standard or Deep. Each is a short warm-up plus blind blocks. Take breaks between blocks whenever you want.
  • Every block starts with a few unscored seconds so your hand can adjust to the new sens.
  • Esc pauses. The block you were in restarts.

    Strafe/Lab

    Calibration

    Each test captures raw mouse counts and timestamps the same way the drills do. Repeat each test 3 times; the spread tells you how much is your hand and how much is the input.

    1 · Ruler DPI test

    Put a ruler beside your mousepad. Line the mouse edge up with 0, start, slide it straight to the distance mark, then press Space or click.

    2 · Physical 360° test

    In the Apex Firing Range, mark two points on your pad that give exactly one 360° turn at your current sens. Here, start, move from mark to mark, then press Space or click. Target: within 1%.

    3 · Input timing test

    Start, then move the mouse in steady circles for 5 seconds. Measures polling rate, whether events are frame-aligned, and integrity counters.
