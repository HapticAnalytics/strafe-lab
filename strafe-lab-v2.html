Strafe/Lab

Tracking sensitivity finder for Apex Legends · MnK

A blind tracking test built around SMG fights. It sweeps you from fast wrist-aim sens down to slow arm-aim sens, records every mouse movement, zooms in on your best zone, and then a tracking coach explains what your aim is doing and what to fix.

Your setup

Current: — cm/360

  • Use your real mouse, mousepad and desk setup. Slow sens blocks need room to move your arm.
  • Turn off Windows "Enhance pointer precision". The test asks the browser for raw input and tells you if it got it.
  • About 11 minutes: a warm-up plus 10 blind blocks. Take breaks between blocks whenever you want.
  • Every block starts with a few unscored seconds so your hand can adjust to the new sens.
  • Esc pauses. The block you were in restarts.
This test needs a mouse. Open this page on your gaming PC.

What you get

Example report

Round one sweeps a wide range from fast to slow. Round two zooms in on your best zone. The peak of the fitted curve is your sens, and the test tells you how confident it is. Then the AI tracking coach reads your mouse data range by range.

Every block runs the same four tracking drills

Point-blank

Building and door fights. ADAD, crouch spam and jumps right in your face. Tests fast reactive tracking.

8 m · 12 s · 30% of score

Close SMG duel

18 m · 12 s · 20% of score

Mid-range

Longer strafes and sprints across your screen. Tests smooth, steady tracking without wobble.

7 m + 10 m · up to 10 s · 25%

2v1 fight

Two strafing enemies with 200 HP each. You deal R-99 damage while on them. Kill one, switch, kill the other.

Enemy movement uses Apex's real speeds: about 4.4 m/s strafing with a gun out, 2 m/s crouch-walking, 7.6 m/s sprinting. Every block replays the exact same movement, so your sensitivity is the only thing that changes.
